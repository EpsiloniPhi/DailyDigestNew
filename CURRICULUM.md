# Curriculum

Rough progression for each track. The daily run picks the next uncovered topic (see `index.md`), and may revisit an earlier topic at greater depth or mix in a related one. Difficulty ramps up over time.

## 1. Probability & Statistics

1. Conditional probability, Bayes, and the law of total probability (beyond textbook level)
2. Linearity of expectation and indicator-variable tricks
3. Conditional expectation as a projection; tower property; law of total variance
4. Generating functions (PGF, MGF) and characteristic functions
5. Inequalities: Markov, Chebyshev, Jensen, Cauchy-Schwarz, Chernoff
6. Order statistics and uniform spacings
7. Convergence modes; LLN; CLT and when it fails
8. Markov chains: hitting times, absorption probabilities, stationary distributions
9. Random walks and gambler's ruin
10. Martingales and the optional stopping theorem
11. Stopping times and Wald's identity
12. Poisson processes and exponential races
13. Brownian motion: construction, reflection principle, first passage times
14. Ito's lemma and stochastic calculus basics
15. Change of measure, Girsanov intuition
16. Estimation: MLE, bias/variance, Cramer-Rao, sufficiency
17. Regression from first principles, multicollinearity, OLS geometry
18. Hypothesis testing, multiple testing, and p-hacking in backtests
19. Covariance matrices, PCA, shrinkage
20. Kelly criterion and optimal betting

## 2. Derivatives

1. Forwards vs futures: cost of carry, convergence, margining
2. Treasuries: bills/notes/bonds, auctions, quoting (32nds), accrued interest
3. Treasury futures: conversion factors, cheapest-to-deliver, delivery options, basis trading
4. Repo, implied repo, and the cash-futures basis trade
5. Interest rate swaps: pricing, DV01, swap spreads
6. Put-call parity and arbitrage bounds
7. Black-Scholes from first principles (replication and risk-neutral pricing)
8. The Greeks: delta, gamma, vega, theta, and their interactions
9. Gamma scalping and the theta-gamma tradeoff
10. Volatility smile/skew and what drives it
11. Spreads: verticals, calendars, diagonals, butterflies, condors
12. Straddles/strangles and trading realized vs implied vol
13. Risk reversals and skew trading
14. FX options: quoting conventions (delta, ATM DNS, 25d RR/BF), premium-adjusted delta
15. Variance and volatility swaps
16. Barrier and digital options; hedging pin risk
17. Rates vol: caps/floors, swaptions
18. Dispersion and correlation trading
19. Convexity: futures vs forward rates, convexity adjustments
20. Exotics and structured products (autocallables, TARFs)

## 3. Algorithms & Data Structures (C++)

1. Arrays and hashing (two-sum family, prefix sums)
2. Two pointers and sliding window
3. Stacks and monotonic stacks
4. Binary search on answers
5. Linked lists
6. Trees: traversals, BST properties, LCA
7. Heaps and priority queues (top-k, median of stream)
8. Intervals and sweep line
9. Graphs: BFS, DFS, topological sort
10. Shortest paths: Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford
11. Union-Find
12. Dynamic programming: 1D, 2D, knapsack, LIS, edit distance
13. Backtracking
14. Tries and string algorithms
15. Bit manipulation
16. Segment trees and Fenwick trees
17. Design problems: LRU cache, order book, rate limiter
18. Randomized algorithms: reservoir sampling, shuffle
19. Concurrency basics in C++ (threads, mutex, atomics)
20. Low-latency C++ concepts (cache locality, move semantics, avoiding allocations)
